Why now

Hello Kitty took decades. The next one won't.

Character IP has always paid off across entertainment, merchandise and licensing. It also used to cost years of animation and production before anyone knew if audiences cared. Generative AI makes characters cheap to create, and social feeds put them in front of millions without a network deal.

Cute is a serious business.

Labubu and Jellycat proved that adults will line up for cute, collectible things without an elaborate backstory. We think characters with real personalities go further. They become entertainment people share, and an identity people wear.
